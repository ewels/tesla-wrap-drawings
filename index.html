How it works

  1. Print a sheet for your car below, or Tesla's own template.
  2. Colour it in. Crayons, pens, paint: anything goes.
  3. Photograph it and drop the photo here. The outline is found and lined up for you.
  4. Download the PNG and send it to the car.

Everything happens in your browser. Photos never leave your device.

Print a colouring sheet

Tesla's template prints the glass roof and gaps as blank paper, so they get coloured in and then thrown away. These sheets hatch them out in light blue, and add registration marks that make the photo line up even more reliably.

Download sheet

Prints best on A4 or Letter, portrait, with "fit to page" on.

Send it to the car

From the Tesla app

Needs app version 4.59 or later. Go to Creations, then Wrap, then Upload, and pick the PNG. On a phone, tap Share and save the image to Photos first.

With a USB drive

Format it as exFAT or FAT32, make a folder called Wraps at the top level and put the PNG inside. Up to 10 wraps at a time.

In the car

Open Toybox, then Paint Shop, then the Wraps tab.

Templates come from teslamotors/custom-wraps; 3D cars are from Tesla’s app, via tesla-wrap.com. Not affiliated with Tesla.
